Scientific second opinion · Nea Makri

A second scientific opinion for complex cases.

Independent assessment and scientific opinion when a diagnosis needs to be confirmed, findings re-evaluated, or a clearer therapeutic plan is required.

When a second opinion is requested

A second opinion is useful when a case remains unclear, when there are conflicting assessments or when the family needs well-documented guidance before deciding on the next step.

  • Review of previous opinions and case history.
  • Targeted re-evaluation with clinical tools.
  • Interpretation of the findings in an understandable way.
  • Suggestions for a therapeutic direction.

What it offers

The process does not simply replace a previous opinion. It checks whether the existing data are sufficient, whether a new assessment is needed and which therapeutic path is most consistent with the findings and the needs of the case.

For which cases

Learning difficulties, dyslexia, neurological disorders, aphasia, voice disorders, dysphagia and cognitive-communication difficulties.
