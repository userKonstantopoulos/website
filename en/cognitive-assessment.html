Memory · Attention · Nea Makri

Cognitive assessment for children, adults and older adults.

Comprehensive assessment of memory, attention, executive function, and cognitive skills in Nea Makri, with a clear scientific interpretation of the findings.

When is it useful

Cognitive assessment is useful when there are difficulties in memory, concentration, organization, and information processing or daily functioning.

  • Assessment of memory and attention.
  • Assessment of executive function.
  • Identification of cognitive strengths and limitations.
  • Recommendations for a therapeutic or supportive plan.

What it clarifies

The assessment helps to see whether the difficulties are mainly related to memory, attention, processing speed, organization, or executive function. This distinction is critical, because different difficulties need different interventions.
