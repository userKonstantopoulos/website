Voice · Dysphagia · Nea Makri

Speech and Language Assessment for Voice and Swallowing Disorders (Dysphagia) in Nea Makri.

If you or someone close to you has persistent hoarseness, voice fatigue, coughing when swallowing, or difficulty with liquids or food, a specialist voice or swallowing assessment may be needed.

What the service includes

The assessment examines voice function, voice quality, swallowing symptoms, and history that may affect safety or daily communication.

  • Clinical assessment of voice.
  • Dysphagia assessment and functional swallowing difficulties.
  • Development of individualized therapeutic exercises and strategies.
  • Clear instructions for the next therapeutic step.

Why is a special assessment needed?

Voice and swallowing directly affect communication, safety, and quality of life. The assessment helps to clarify whether the difficulty concerns vocal use, muscle function, a neurological factor, or a combination of these.

When to request an assessment

When there is persistent hoarseness, voice fatigue, change in voice quality, coughing during swallowing, or difficulty with liquids and foods.
