Clinical and Academic Profile

Dr Konstantinos Konstantopoulos is Professor in the Department of Speech and Language Therapy at the University of the Peloponnese. He has extensive clinical, academic, and research experience in the assessment and management of communication, language, speech, and swallowing disorders.

He holds a Master's degree from Washington State University (USA) and a PhD from University College London (United Kingdom). His doctoral studies were supported by a prestigious Marie Curie Fellowship of the European Union.

Dr Konstantopoulos has extensive long-term clinical experience in Speech Therapy in both Greece and Cyprus. His academic work includes research, university teaching, and scientific publications in the fields of assessment and rehabilitation of communication disorders.

At his private practice, he provides comprehensive assessment and intervention services based on evidence-based practice. Every treatment plan is individually tailored to ensure accurate diagnosis, effective intervention, and meaningful improvements in communication, functional independence, and quality of life.
