Assessment · Documentation · Intervention

Assessment precedes therapy.

The method is based on systematic investigation, clear interpretation of the findings and a therapeutic plan adapted to the person.

01Case history

Recording of difficulties, goals, previous assessments and everyday needs.

02Targeted assessment

Use of clinical tools according to age, difficulty and the referral question.

03Interpretation of findings

Clear information about what the results show and what they mean functionally.

04Therapeutic plan

Specific goals, session frequency and progress monitoring.

05Collaboration

Where needed, coordination with family, educators or caregivers.

06Re-evaluation

The plan is adjusted based on progress and functional needs.

07Individualized approach

Each therapeutic plan is designed separately, based on each person's age, difficulty and functional goal, and is continuously adjusted according to progress.
