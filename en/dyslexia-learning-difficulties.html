Dyslexia · Learning disabilities · Nea Makri

Assessment of dyslexia and learning difficulties in Nea Makri.

Specialized investigation of reading, language and cognitive skills with modern tools, for children and teenagers from Nea Makri, Marathon, Rafina and Eastern Attica.

What the assessment includes

The assessment is not limited to a general picture of school performance. It examines individual skills related to reading, spelling, comprehension, phonological processing, working memory and attention.

  • A detailed history is taken and discussed with the family.
  • Targeted testing for learning and language skills.
  • Use of eye-tracking technology when clinically indicated.
  • Clear communication of findings and next steps.

How Assessment Helps

After assessment, the family has a clearer picture of whether the difficulty is related to decoding, reading fluency, spelling, language processing, or cognitive factors. This allows for a more targeted intervention and avoids general exercises that do not address the real cause.

Why early assessment is important

A proper assessment helps to distinguish dyslexia from other learning, language, or cognitive difficulties. This way, the treatment plan becomes specific, with goals that can be monitored in practice.
