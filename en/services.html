Speech Therapy in Nea Makri

Assessment and speech therapy intervention services.

At MEMORY & SPEECH we provide specialized speech therapy services for children, adults and older adults in Nea Makri, with access from Marathon, Rafina, Mati, Pikermi and Eastern Attica.

How the services are organized

Assessment first, then targeted intervention.

Every service starts with a clear clinical investigation. The goal is to identify what really challenges the child, adult or older adult, and to design an intervention that can be applied in practice.

What you receive from the assessment

  • A clear picture of the skills that need support.
  • Scientific interpretation of the findings without vague generalities.
  • A proposed therapeutic plan with practical goals.
01Dyslexia and learning difficulties

Detailed investigation of reading, spelling, comprehension, phonological processing and cognitive factors that affect learning.

See details 02Cognitive assessment

Assessment of memory, attention, executive functions and the overall cognitive profile for children, adults and older adults.

See details 03Second opinion

Independent scientific re-evaluation when there are doubts, conflicting opinions or a need for clear therapeutic planning.

See details 04Neurological disorders

Intervention for communication and swallowing difficulties after a stroke, traumatic brain injury or degenerative neurological condition, as well as aphasia, dysarthria and apraxia of speech.

See details 05Voice and swallowing

Clinical investigation of voice difficulties and dysphagia, aiming at safety, functionality and proper guidance.
