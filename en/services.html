Speech Therapy in Nea Makri

Assessment and Speech Therapy services.

MEMORY & SPEECH provides Specialized Speech Therapy Services for children, adults, and older adults in Nea Makri, Marathonas, Rafina, Mati, Pikermi, and the Eastern Attica area.

How the services are provided

Comprehensive assessment followed by targeted speech therapy.

Each service starts with a detailed clinical assessment. The goal is to identify the factors underlying the difficulties experienced by the child, adult, or older adult and to design an intervention that can be implemented in everyday practice.

What you will get from the assessment

  • A clear picture of the skills that need support.
  • Clear scientific interpretation of the findings without vague or generalized comments.
  • Proposed treatment plan with practical goals.
01Dyslexia and Learning Difficulties

Specialist speech and language assessment when a child or teenager has persistent difficulties with reading, spelling, comprehension or learning.
